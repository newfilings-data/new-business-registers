# New DOT Numbers — 2026-09

Monthly open data from the independent directory at https://newdotnumbers.com.
12244 rows in `companies.csv`.

## Columns

- `name` — firm name
- `register_number` — register or filing number, as published
- `legal_form` — legal form, when the source states one
- `founding_date` — the date shown on the firm page (registration or official publication, `YYYY-MM-DD`)
- `city` — city, when published
- `region` — region, province, or state, when published
- `url` — firm page on this directory

Natural persons and sole traders are left out. Street addresses, telephone and fax numbers, email addresses, officer names, and insurance policy numbers are not in this file.

## Source

FMCSA Company Census File on data.transportation.gov, dataset az4n-8mr2 (https://data.transportation.gov/Trucking-and-Motorcoaches/Company-Census-File/az4n-8mr2).

## Licence

U.S. government public data. The dataset metadata has no licence identifier. Completeness is not guaranteed and the data are not legal advice. Policy numbers are not included.
