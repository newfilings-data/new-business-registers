# Ohio New Businesses — 2026-09

Monthly open data from the independent directory at https://ohionewbusinesses.com.
824 rows in `companies.csv`.

## Columns

- `name` — firm name
- `register_number` — register or filing number, as published
- `legal_form` — legal form, when the source states one
- `founding_date` — the date shown on the firm page (registration or official publication, `YYYY-MM-DD`)
- `city` — city, when published
- `region` — region, province, or state, when published
- `url` — firm page on this directory

Natural persons and sole traders are left out. Street addresses, telephone and fax numbers, email addresses, officer names, and insurance policy numbers are not in this file.

## Source

Free official files that name Ohio: FMCSA Company Census (az4n-8mr2), SEC EDGAR Form D, CMS NPPES weekly dissemination, Ohio Department of Taxation active sales-tax accounts, and, when a file is imported, Ohio Secretary of State monthly files. SAM.gov is included only when a key is configured.
